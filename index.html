Egocentric Spatial Reasoning in Embodied MLLMs Dissertation
Benchmarking 3D spatial reasoning in vision-language models. The central question: do current VLMs maintain a coherent model of 3D space as they move through an environment, or do they collapse to frame-level perception when reasoning requires integrating observations across viewpoints, time, and scale? Supervised by Dr Rolandos Potamias, Imperial College London.

Agentic Framework for Failures in Vision Foundation Models
A system for detecting, diagnosing, and explaining CLIP failures. Combines representation-level interpretability, explaining why CLIP fails on individual images, with an agentic routing pipeline that decides how to handle each failure class.

World Action Models for Robotic Manipulation
Exploring world models as action-conditioned latent state representations for robotic manipulation. Compares 4D reconstruction and video-based scene representations as learned dynamics priors, evaluating how well they capture the geometry and motion needed to predict the consequences of an agent's actions in a manipulation setting.
