My research interests lie in spatial and 3D scene understanding, vision-language models, and embodied AI — particularly how multimodal models reason about space, viewpoint, and time.

Egocentric Embodied Benchmark for Spatial Reasoning in MLLMs Dissertation
Benchmarking 3D spatial reasoning in vision-language models using egocentric video from Meta's Project Aria glasses. The central question: do current VLMs maintain a coherent model of 3D space as they move through an environment, or do they collapse to frame-level perception when reasoning requires integrating observations across viewpoints, time, and scale? Supervised by Dr Rolandos Potamias, Imperial College London.
